# Brand Confusion — Remediation Briefing for Prima Assicurazioni / Hello Prima

**Prepared by:** Prima Financial Services Ltd t/a Prima Insurance Brokers (FCA FRN 842275)
**Date of underlying review:** 2 July 2026 (all observations captured with full-page
screenshots and archived HTML; captures available on request)
**Purpose:** provided at your request, to identify where your customers encounter our
business when looking for yours, so that remediation can be put in place. This document
is factual and is provided to assist remediation; it is without prejudice to the wider
discussions between us.

---

## 1. The mechanism in one paragraph

Your customers do not know your brand as "Hello Prima" — they know it as "Prima" or
"Prima insurance" (in a sample of 950 of your Trustpilot reviews, one reviewer used
"Hello Prima"; the rest used "Prima", "Prima insurance" or "Prima car insurance").
When they need to contact you — claims, complaints, cancellations, mid-term changes —
they search those terms. Because your published contact routes are hard to find and
your own pages have historically been titled "Prima Insurance", the top results with a
visible phone number are ours. The result: our office currently receives **30–50
misdirected telephone calls per day** intended for you, plus a continual stream of
misdirected emails and post, and we have received formal complaints, negative reviews,
correspondence from solicitors and the police, and County Court Judgments relating to
your policies.

### 2.2 Google Business Profile / Google Maps

- Both businesses' Google listings are named identically **"Prima Insurance"**.
- Our listing displays +44 330 088 1135. **Your listing displays no phone number at
  all** — Google publicly invites anyone to "Add place's phone number" (an open
  crowd-edit invitation on your profile).
- A customer searching "Prima Insurance" on Google/Maps for their car insurer's number
  can be shown only our number.

### 2.3 AI answers and answer boxes

- Bing's AI-generated answer for "prima insurance claims number" cites
  helloprima.co.uk/claims **and** primainsurance.co.uk/contact as its two sources for
  one "Prima Insurance" — your claims number is being sourced-mixed with our contact page.
- AI search summaries observed during the review attributed "0330 088 1135 … Prima
  House, 21 Bury New Road, Prestwich" (our details) **to helloprima.co.uk**, and
  described our numbers as "the official Prima car insurance contact numbers for the UK".

### 2.4 Your own website (root cause of much of the above)

- Your page titles used the exact phrase **"Prima Insurance"** continuously from
  December 2023 to at least October 2025 ("About Prima Insurance - Prima Insurance",
  "Making a Motor Insurance Claim - Prima Insurance", including the 404 page). These
  title tags are what search engines display and match our trading name.
- Your main site has never had a /contact or /complaints page (contact details live
  only on a help-centre subdomain), and the published phone number was removed from
  your "How can I contact you?" article during 2024. Customers who cannot find a
  number on your site search the open web — see 2.1.

### 2.5 Review platforms

- Trustpilot's own company search for "prima insurance" returns **our profile first**;
  yours appears seventh. Your Trustpilot business name displays as simply "Prima".
- Your reviewers repeatedly describe searching for a phone number ("There's no phone
  number to call which is worrying"; "I…googled you").
- A review has been posted on our Trustpilot profile that appears on its face to
  concern a motor policy of the type you underwrite; we also hold formal complaints
  and reviews received directly that relate to your policies.
- A public Google review on your own profile states the reviewer "called a company
  called Prima, who advised me to search for Hello Prima".

### 2.6 Price-comparison sites

Go.Compare lists you as "Prima Car Insurance"; Confused.com and Uswitch list "Prima" /
"Prima Essentials" / "Prima Plus" / "Prima Premier". None uses "Hello Prima", none
shows any contact details, and none disambiguates from us — customers are handed the
bare word "Prima" and left to search (see 2.1).

### 2.7 Directories and third-party "contact" sites

- Directory listings exist for your business under the name "Prima Insurance, London"
  (e.g. Cylex) with no phone number, alongside our long-standing listings under
  "Prima Insurance"/"Prima Insurance Brokers" with our numbers.
- Third-party SEO "contact directory" pages actively misroute your customers: a live
  page titled "Prima Car Insurance Contact UK 2026: Phone, Claims & Complaints"
  (kaeltripton.com) instructs readers to use **primainsurance.co.uk** — our website —
  for your customer service, 24/7 claims and complaints, and never mentions
  helloprima.co.uk. A claims-management site (claimsnumber.com) advertises its own
  0333 number as the "Prima Car Insurance Accident Claim" number.
- Your group X/Twitter handle is @Prima_Insurance (our trading name verbatim); your
  UK operation has no UK-facing social profiles, while our profiles publish our
  contact details.

## 3. Remediation actions within your control

Offered constructively; you will have your own views on feasibility.

1. **Publish a findable UK phone number** — on your main site (a /contact page on
   helloprima.co.uk itself, not only the help subdomain), on your Google Business
   Profile, and in your aggregator listings. This single change addresses the root
   cause: customers search the open web *because* no number is findable.
2. **Fix your Google Business Profile**: correct the name (it should not read "Prima
   Insurance"), add your phone number, and claim/verify it so the public cannot edit it.
3. **Retitle your web pages** so they no longer use "Prima Insurance" (our trading
   name) in titles/meta descriptions, and target your own brand ("Prima" / "Hello
   Prima") plus disambiguating copy ("Prima — car insurance, helloprima.co.uk").
4. **SEO for your own contact intent**: create and index pages answering "contact",
   "claims number", "complaints", "cancel my policy" queries so your pages, not ours,
   rank for them.
5. **Aggregator listings**: ask Go.Compare, Confused.com, Uswitch and Compare the
   Market to display your brand consistently with a contact route (or link), so
   post-sale customers are not left searching a bare name.
6. **Third-party clean-up**: takedown/correction requests to the misrouting contact
   pages and claims-number sites in 2.7, and correction of the Cylex-type listings.
7. **A disambiguation notice** on your site and help centre ("We are not connected to
   Prima Insurance Brokers of Manchester — if you are looking for them, see
   primainsurance.co.uk", and we would publish the reciprocal), which also gives the
   AI answer engines clean source text to stop conflating the two firms.
8. **The interim contact route discussed at our meeting**: a dedicated phone number,
   email address and named escalation contact to which we can direct the 30–50
   misdirected callers per day and forward misdirected correspondence. We ask that
   this be confirmed in writing, with an agreed process that is compliant with UK GDPR
   (given callers disclose personal, claims and payment information).
