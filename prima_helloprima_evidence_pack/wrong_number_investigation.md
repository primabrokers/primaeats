# Wrong-Number Investigation — 0330 088 1135 and 0161 826 1620

**Question:** Does either Prima Insurance Brokers phone number appear anywhere publicly
in connection with Hello Prima / Prima car insurance?

**Date of investigation:** 2 July 2026.
**Primary detail:** `agent_reports/agent1b_number_searches.md` (findings NUM-01–NUM-18),
supported by TP-20, DIR-11, SOC-09, WB-07, AGG-12, SE-07, AI-15, DIR-12.

## Verdicts

### 0330 088 1135 (broker main line)
**Direct attribution to Hello Prima: NOT CONFIRMED.**
No public page was found that publishes, suggests or repeats 0330 088 1135 as a Hello
Prima / Prima car insurance number. Every indexed occurrence belongs to the broker
family (primainsurance.co.uk; directory entries for "Prima Insurance Brokers" / "Prima
Protect Insurance Brokers", Bury New Road, Prestwich).

### 0161 826 1620 (broker complaints/privacy line)
**Direct attribution to Hello Prima: NOT CONFIRMED.**
Only substantive indexed sources: the broker's own terms page (published as the
complaints/privacy line of Prima Financial Services Ltd) and broker directory entries
(Feefo, BIBA, yably, Cylex). Zero results for every Hello Prima combination.

### Critical qualification — exposure and machine attribution ARE confirmed
The "not confirmed" verdicts above concern *static publication of a wrong association*.
Three related phenomena **were** confirmed and captured:

1. **SERP exposure (Category I/A-exposure):** the broker's numbers are the only phone
   numbers rendered on the results page for insurer-intent searches — #1 for "prima
   insurance phone number" on DuckDuckGo and Bing with 0330 088 1135 in the snippet
   (SE-01/SE-02), 0161 826 1620 in the snippet for complaints queries (SE-04, AI-06),
   and visible even for "hello prima contact number"-type queries (SE-03, AI-04).
2. **Machine-generated wrong attribution (live demonstrations):** an AI search summary
   attributed "0330 088 1135 … Prima House, 21 Bury New Road, Prestwich" **to
   helloprima.co.uk** (SE-07); another asserted the 0330 number "is indeed associated
   with Hello Prima's car insurance" (DIR-12); a controlled test produced "Main Customer
   Service Number: 0330 088 1135 … These are the official Prima car insurance contact
   numbers for the UK" (AI-15). Bing's AI answer box cites the broker's contact page as
   a source about the motor insurer (AI-03). These are transient, machine-generated
   outputs — captured verbatim with screenshots — not static pages.
3. **Google Maps asymmetry:** two listings both named "Prima Insurance"; only the
   broker's shows a number (+44 330 088 1135); Hello Prima's shows none (REV-01/02,
   AI-07–09).

## Overall statement

As of 2 July 2026, **no public page or snippet was found showing 0330 088 1135 or
0161 826 1620 published in connection with Hello Prima / Prima car insurance**
(verdict: not confirmed). What the evidence does establish — with screenshots — is
that (a) both firms surface together for virtually all "Prima insurance" contact
searches, (b) the broker's numbers are the only numbers a searcher sees, (c) AI answer
systems have generated the wrong association on demand, and (d) at least one customer
publicly reports phoning "a company called Prima" by mistake while seeking Hello Prima.
This does not prove customers dial the broker's numbers; it documents the mechanism by
which they plausibly would. Completed misdirected contact would need first-party
evidence (call logs, recordings, misdirected correspondence).

**Unverifiable lead (recorded honestly):** a ceoemail.com page for "Prima Subsidiary
Ltd" appeared in one result list for a 0161-number query but is bot-gated and not
archived; it could not be inspected (NUM report, blockers).
