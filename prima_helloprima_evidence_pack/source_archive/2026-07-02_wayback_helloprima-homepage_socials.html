Our insurance policy

Covers you for all the important stuff

What’s included?

Our comprehensive car insurance is designed to put you at ease.

  • If your car’s damaged, we’ll repair it up to its market value and provide a courtesy car.

  • If your car’s stolen, we’ll replace it or pay its market value.

  • If an accident’s your fault, we’ll pay up to £20,000,000.

  • If you drive abroad in the EU, you’ll have full cover for 90 days.

  • If you’re seriously injured, we’ll pay £5,000.

  • If you drive someone else's car, we'll provide third party cover, so long as you're eligible.

What’s not included?

We can’t cover everything. But we make it clear that you won’t be covered if…

  • Your car’s driven by someone not named on your policy.

  • You drive without a valid driving licence.

  • You drive under the influence of drink or drugs.

  • Your car’s left unlocked or with the keys inside.

  • You put in the wrong fuel.

  • You drive outside the UK or EU.
