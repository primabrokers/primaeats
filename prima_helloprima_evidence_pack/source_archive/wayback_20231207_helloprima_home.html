What’s included?

Our comprehensive car insurance cover is simple, clear and designed to put you at ease.

  • If your car’s damaged
    we’ll repair it up to its market value and provide a courtesy car.

  • If your car’s stolen
    we’ll replace it or pay its market value.

  • If an accident’s your fault
    we’ll pay up to £20,000,000.

  • If you drive other cars
    they’ll have third party cover too.

  • If you drive abroad in Europe
    you’ll have full cover for 90 days.

  • If you’re seriously injured
    we’ll pay £5,000.

What’s not included?

We can’t cover you for everything. But we make it clear that you won’t be covered if…

  1. Your car’s driven by someone not named on your policy.

  2. You drive without a valid driving licence.

  3. You drive under the influence of drink or drugs.

  4. Your car’s left unlocked or with the keys inside.

  5. You put in the wrong fuel.

  6. You drive outside the UK or EU.

You’ll find full details of what is and isn’t covered when you get your quote.
