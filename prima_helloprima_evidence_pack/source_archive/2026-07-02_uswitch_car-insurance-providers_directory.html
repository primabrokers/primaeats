Car insurance providers FAQs

Which car insurance provider is best for me?

There isn’t one single “best” car insurance provider. There are many different insurance types, and different things are important to different people. For instance, you might be solely motivated by price, but someone else might be motivated by good customer service. This is why providing as much information as possible helps us find the right policy for you.

Why are some car insurance providers listed more than once?

Some car insurance providers offer different types of policy, depending on the level of cover that is needed.

How do I find out my current car insurance provider?

There are a few steps you can take to help find out which provider you are insured under. 

  • Search your emails to find any policy documents or communication with policy providers.
  • Check your paperwork. There may be a letter or hard copy of documents in the glove compartment or anywhere else you file your documents.
  • Give your bank a call. It may be able to share information on which policy provider you are making your insurance payments to. 
  • The Motor Insurance Database can check if a car is insured, all you need is your car registration to search the website for the car’s details.

How do I change my car insurance provider?

It’s a simple task to change your car insurance provider - all you need to do is gather all the relevant details about your car and driving history and use a service like Uswitch to find a new policy. Once you’ve done so, you can cancel your existing policy, but remember that you may have to pay a cancellation fee.
