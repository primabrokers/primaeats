Leading car insurance companies in the UK

The UK car insurance market is estimated to be worth around £16 billion in written premiums, according to BeInsure.

The leading car insurers in the UK feature some familiar names, like Admiral, Hastings and Direct Line.

The top 10 insurers listed below control over 70% of the car insurance market between them. These established brands insure large numbers of UK drivers and use their reputation and advertising power to attract customers.

What you might not realise is that many of them also own and operate a variety of smaller brands within their insurance portfolio. This allows them to target different sections of the car insurance market, like younger drivers, telematics drivers, and so on.

Some brands build market share by concentrating on niche sub-sections of cars and drivers, like temporary cover, modifications or convicted drivers.

Other companies trade on their reputation for great customer service, quick and reasonable claims processing, and so on.

Finally, we’re starting to see more technology-driven disrupters using things like AI to break into this lucrative market.

Types of cover offered by UK car insurance providers

Third Party Only

Not every insurer offers third party only cover. This is one of the reasons it often costs more than both TPFT and fully comprehensive (see below).

Third party insurance is the minimum you need to drive legally on UK roads - it's designed to protect other road users if you’re involved in an accident. It doesn’t cover you or your car for injury or damage.

Learn more about third party insurance

Third Party, Fire and Theft

Third party, fire and theft (TPFT) cover is the same as third party only cover with regard to a collision, and you are also covered if your car is stolen or catches fire.

Many insurers offer TPFT cover and it's currently the cheapest option for many drivers according to our car insurance cost data.

Learn more about third party, fire and theft insurance

Fully Comprehensive

Comprehensive cover offers all the third party protection you need to drive on the road. The difference is that it covers you for collisions that you cause, or that are deemed to be your fault. You also have fire and theft cover, and most comprehensive policies will cover things like windscreen damage, personal belongings, accidents involving uninsured drivers, and more as standard.

Learn more about comprehensive car insurance

Add-ons and Optional Extras

For anything that's not usually covered as standard, like legal expenses or breakdown cover, you can usually add these to your policy for an additional fee. Not all providers supply every add-on though, so make sure that the provider you choose offers the extras you need before you buy.

How to choose a car insurance company for your cover

With so many car insurers available the choice can be overwhelming. It's essential to go in with an idea of what’s important to you and identify an insurer that fits the bill.

Compare price and policy features

Your car insurance quotes are sorted by price, and you can select policy features that are important to you with just a couple of clicks on the results page.

Select policy extras like breakdown cover or legal expenses and see which insurers include them as standard and which will charge you an additional fee.

You can also dig deeper into each policy by clicking on the quote that interests you.

Check reviews and reputation

We’ll show you the Defaqto rating for every policy that we offer on your results page.

Policies with a higher Defaqto star rating offer more comprehensive features and benefits - so it’s always worth balancing cost with the quality of the cover.

You can also visit websites like Trustpilot to find out what real customers think about the insurers, their customer service, and their all-important claims services.

Understand the excess and policy terms

Insurance can be complicated with jargon and small print all over the place. Do read through the policy terms and conditions, and don’t be afraid to ask your insurer questions about anything you don’t understand.

Your compulsory and voluntary excesses are particularly important to understand. The total of these is the amount you must pay towards the cost of the claim. So while increasing your voluntary excess can reduce your premium, not being able to afford to pay the excess in the event of a claim could leave you without a car.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the best car insurance companies in the UK?

Well obviously it’s… no, it’s not our job to decide which insurer is best for you. Everyone has different needs when buying car insurance and, as with any financial choice, it's your budget, your requirements, and your personal circumstances that should guide your choice.

If price is your main concern then there are stripped down ‘essentials’ and ‘basic’ policies that might suit you. Make sure they cover everything you expect, though.

If you have a large no-claims discount, you might look for an insurer that offers a higher discount, or that includes NCD protection with their cover.

And if you’re a younger driver, you might be looking for telematics cover while you build experience.

For each of these examples, a different insurer and policy might be ‘best’ in each case.

Who is the cheapest car insurance company?

There’s no one ‘cheapest’ car insurer as providers give you individual quotes depending on your situation.

This means the amount they charge you for your insurance could be very different for someone else in different circumstances. So, there’s no cheaper car insurance company overall.

The best way to find the cheapest insurance for you is to gather quotes from many providers and weigh up the costs against the level of cover they offer.

Could I save money by switching car insurance provider?

Yes, absolutely. When you get your renewal quote from your current car insurance provider, there’s nothing stopping you from getting quotes from other providers to compare. You might find the same level of cover for a cheaper price with a different provider.

Do all car insurance providers offer comprehensive cover?

Yes. All of the insurers on our panel offer comprehensive cover, and we’re not aware of any providers who only deal in third party car insurance.

Can I switch car insurance providers at any time?

Yes. You can switch car insurers at any time, and you don’t have to wait for your policy renewal. If you cancel outside your cooling-off period, you’ll likely have to pay a cancellation fee.

Will comparing car insurance companies affect my credit score?

No. Getting a car insurance quote and comparing companies will not affect your credit score, although how you choose to pay for your policy might.

When you get a quote from us we’ll carry out a soft search, which won’t be visible to lenders or banks and won’t affect your credit score.

If you pay annually you won’t be checked because there's no credit agreement - you’ve paid the full sum upfront.

If you choose to pay monthly, you’re entering into a credit agreement, so an insurer will make a hard search. This will appear on your credit record and could affect your credit score, particularly if you’ve had a few hard searches in a short time period.
