Cookies are small text files that can be used by websites to make a user's experience more efficient. On this page you can find out more about the cookies installed, their purpose and retention times. Necessary cookies are always enabled, but you can choose whether to enable preference, marketing and statistic cookies. To enable or disable cookies, simply click on the relevant checkbox in the cookie banner settings. Under Regulation 6 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 ("PECR"), it is necessary for us to collect consent from all our users in order to implement all types of cookies except those that are not strictly necessary for the operation of this site. For all other types of cookies, i.e. those that are not strictly necessary, we need your permission.

 

This site uses different types of cookies (listed below). Some cookies are placed by third party services that appear on our pages.

 

You can, at any time, change or withdraw your consent from the Cookie Banner on our website by clicking the green cookie icon.

 

Types of cookies:


Necessary cookies introduction
Necessary cookies help make a website usable by enabling basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

 

Preference cookies introduction

Preference cookies enable a website to remember information that changes the way the website behaves or looks, like your preferred language or the region that you are in.

 

Statistics cookies introduction

Statistic cookies help website owners to understand how visitors interact with websites by collecting and reporting information anonymously. These may also be known as analytic cookies.

 

Marketing cookies introduction

Marketing cookies are used to track visitors across websites. The intention is to display ads that are relevant and engaging for the individual user and thereby more valuable for publishers and third party advertisers.

 

Similar Technologies 

At Prima, we use ‘similar technologies’ alongside cookies to analyse how you use our website. We use these to better understand your user journey, so that we can improve your experience when using our website. An example of this is our use of ‘CashGrabber’ which is used to monitor your experience when purchasing a policy through our website. 

The information that CashGrabber collects includes: 

  • Information on when a button is clicked; 
  • Time stamps; 
  • Information on the payment link used; 
  • Your session ID; 
  • Any offer IDs used; 
  • Your device type; and 
  • Your browser version. 

It is through the use of CashGrabber that we will conduct analysis on the above data. 

 

If you have any queries concerning our use of your personal information, please email us at privacy-uk@prima.it or email our DPO at admin@digitallawuk.com.

 

Email Tracking
At Prima, we may use tracking tools, like Zendesk, to check if some service emails are received and opened. For example, emails around updating the personal information on your insurance policy. This is because these emails are vital for carrying out our services and meeting our legal and regulatory obligations

 

Managing cookies through your browser settings

In addition to the functionalities made available directly by Prima (banner and cookie policy), it is possible to choose, at any time, whether to accept or refuse the activation of cookies by changing the settings of your browser and selecting which cookies to disable. Deleting or disabling technical cookies, however, could make it impossible to use the site, view its contents or use its services. For more information on how to set your cookie preferences, you can refer to the instructions of your individual browser:

  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Google Chrome
  • Safari

Your preferences for the use of cookies on the Website are in turn recorded in a special cookie. In the event that this cookie does not work properly, we recommend that you delete any non-required cookies (i.e. those that are not strictly necessary) and restrict their use through your browser options.

 

To prevent Google Analytics from using your Data, you can use the browser add-on that disables the Google Analytics JavaScript code (ga.js, analytics.js, dc.js).
