Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the best car insurance provider?

There’s lots of things to consider when looking for the best car insurance for you. You’ll want to weigh up the cost and the amount of cover you get, plus any add-ons you need.

You should also check the insurance provider. Have a look at what their score is on websites like Trustpilot, and see what kind of experiences other customers have with them. You can also check any awards they have, what their policies score on websites like Defaqto, and if they’re registered and regulated by the FCA.

Who is the cheapest car insurance company?

Insurance providers give individual quotes depending on your situation. This means what they charge you for your insurance could be very different for someone else in different circumstances.

This means there’s no cheaper car insurance company overall. The best way to find the cheapest insurance for you is to gather quotes from many providers and weigh up the costs against the amount of cover they offer.

Could I save money by switching car insurance provider?

Absolutely. When you get your renewal quote from your current car insurance provider, there’s nothing stopping you from getting quotes from other providers to compare. You might find the same level of cover for a cheaper price with a different provider.

Why are some insurance companies listed more than once?

Some insurance companies are listed more than once because they offer more than one policy for you to compare.

These might include:

  • Telematics policies which monitor your driving ability
  • Tiered policies with more extras included as standard the higher up you go
  • Policies only available through price comparison websites separate to their regular policies
