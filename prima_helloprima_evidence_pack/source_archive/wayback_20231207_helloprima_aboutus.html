A growing favourite among drivers

Our story began in Italy in 2015.

We decided to make car insurance simpler for drivers. That meant no confusing small print and claims procedures. Just honest and affordable cover, you could actually rely on.

Today over 2 million drivers count on Prima. And following our success in Italy, we’re now bringing our insurance to the UK.
