Our mission

Putting drivers first

“For us, Prima has always been about putting you first.

We started out in Italy, because we didn’t like the way traditional companies were doing car insurance. The policies were confusing. The prices were high. The service often felt cold and slow.

So we decide to change things, by combining time-saving, cost-cutting technology with a friendly, human touch.

Now we bring millions of drivers across Europe a great experience at a great price. And I’m excited to be leading our UK team based in London, as we set out to help you and millions more.”

Nick Ielpo,
Prima UK Country Manager

Our story

We’re just getting started

We’ve already made big changes in the world of insurance. And we’re not stopping yet.

  • 2015

    We launch in Italy

    We set out to change car insurance and provide drivers with a great experience at a great price.

  • 2018

    €100M gets invested in us

    Thanks to our early success, we win the support of big name investors: Goldman Sachs and Blackstone.

  • 2020

    We celebrate 1M drivers

    In just five years, we earn the trust of over a million drivers and become Italy’s number one online insurance provider.

  • 2022

    We take Prima global

    We set up our own teams in the UK and Spain, to help millions more drivers across Europe.

Backed by global giants

In 2018, we received over €100 million of investment thanks to Goldman Sachs and Blackstone. And in 2022, Carlyle also joined our shareholders. With their support, we’re growing fast.
