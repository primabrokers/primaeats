FAQs

How to choose the right insurance provider for you? 

When choosing a provider, the cost of your policy will be a major factor. While it’s often easier to choose the cheapest cover, you might be doing your wallet more harm than good in the long run. Finding the right policy for your needs is the best approach.  

Things to consider when choosing your insurance: 

  1. The type of cover you want  

The type of coverage you choose for your car will affect the overall cost of your insurance policy. Most insurers provide various levels of coverage along with additional policy extras. If you opt for the highest level of coverage and add-ons such as breakdown protection, your insurance costs will be higher. It is recommended that you always choose the level of cover you need for your own circumstances.  

  1. Your own personal needs 

Do you need a specialised insurance policy? Or are you driving a modified vehicle? All of these factors can influence the cost of your policy. Like your age, the type of car you’re insuring is also taken into account. Whether you’re seeking over-70s motor insurance or a young driver’s policy, our panel of insurers allows you to find the right provider for your specific needs. 

What is the cheapest car insurance company in the UK? 

Insurance rates can vary frequently during the year, meaning that the cheapest option for one driver may not be the most affordable for another. That’s why, when you compare quotes with Quotezone, our system arranges your quotes starting with the least expensive policy. This helps you find the best coverage to match your needs and budget. For more information on how insurance rates have changed over the year, read our report on the average price of insurance this year.
