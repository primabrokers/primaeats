## 1. Inability to contact Hello Prima (Category E — Confirmed, repeated, first-person)

Trustpilot (helloprima.co.uk; 5,796 reviews, TrustScore 4.3; 456 English 1★ + 73 2★):

- **"I…googled you"** — a customer explicitly went searching the open web for Prima's
  contact details after buying via Confused.com (4★, 5 Nov 2024, TP-05). A second
  googling instance: reviewer "Vannin", Oct 2024.
- **"There's no phone number to call which is worrying"** (1★, 2 Jun 2026, TP-07).
- **"at first I couldn't find a number to actually speak to a person"** (5★, 19 Feb
  2026, TP-06).
- Five separate reviews state there is "no phone number" / "couldn't find a number"
  (TP-06–TP-10); further recurring themes: no weekend phone cover, chatbot/WhatsApp-only
  routes (TP-04–TP-12, TP-17).

Google reviews on Hello Prima's own profile (REV-04–REV-09): "They do not answer the
phone", "you can't even phone them", "AI phone chatbots", "utterly useless over the
phone".

Historical corroboration (WB-05, WB-06): Hello Prima removed "Call us 02033189745" from
its "How can I contact you?" help article between 21 Feb and 11 Sep 2024, and its main
site has never had an archived /contact or /complaints page.

**Why this matters:** every such customer is pushed into an open-web search whose
results are dominated by the broker's contact details (see
`search_journey_report.md`). This is the misdirection mechanism, evidenced end-to-end.

## 2. "Wrong Prima" / "different Prima" evidence (Category D)

- **REV-03 (Confirmed, direct):** 5★ Google review on Hello Prima's own profile
  (reviewer "eleanor burnet", ~2 yrs ago): *"I initially tried to find a phone number
  and called a company called Prima, who advised me to search for Hello Prima. All I
  could find was live chat or email…"* — a customer phoned a different "Prima" while
  hunting for Hello Prima. The review does not identify which Prima was dialled.
- **TP-01 (Probable):** the only review on the broker's Trustpilot page
  (primainsurance.co.uk; 1★, 10 Jun 2026) reads as a motor policyholder's complaint
  ("refused to change [policy dates]. Charged me for changing address… Sent my proof of
  NCB more times than I can count") — matching Hello Prima's 1★ pattern point-for-point.
  Probable misdirected review; not confirmable from public data (the broker also
  arranges insurance).
- **TP-20 / SOC-09 (negative, stated plainly):** across 950 Trustpilot reviews and all
  indexed social platforms, no post uses "wrong Prima" / "different Prima" / "another
  Prima" or reports dialling the broker's numbers.

## 3. Whose reviews surface where (Category I)

- Trustpilot's own company search for "prima insurance" returns **the broker's profile
  first**; Hello Prima appears seventh. For bare "prima", Hello Prima is first — the
  inversion happens exactly when a customer adds the word "insurance" (TP-03).
- The broker's Trustpilot profile is claimed but populated with placeholder junk
  ("aaa", a@gmail.com, 07891544866) — it gives a landing customer no correct broker
  contact details and no signal distinguishing it from Hello Prima (TP-02).
- Bing "prima insurance brokers manchester reviews" ranks **Hello Prima's Trustpilot
  page #1**, above the broker's own site and Feefo listing (REV-13) — misdirection runs
  in both directions.
- Consumers' shorthand for the insurer is the broker's name: of 950 reviews, 1 says
  "Hello Prima"; 17 say "Prima insurance"; 9 "Prima car insurance"; the rest "Prima"
  (TP-19). Trustpilot displays the insurer's business name as simply "Prima".

## 4. Claims routing (Category F)

- Hello Prima claims are handled by third party **Broker Direct Plc**; 1★ reviewers rail
  against "Prima Insurance and Broker Direct", blurring the word "broker" around the
  Prima brand (TP-15, TP-16; corroborated by a Mar 2025 archived review, WB-12).
- claimsnumber.com (an admitted independent claims-management company) publishes "Prima
  Car Insurance Accident Claim Contact Phone Number 0333 006 4465" — neither Prima's
  number (AGG-09, AI-12); prima.theclaimscentre.net (Bolton 01204 numbers) also ranks
  for Prima claims queries (SE-10).
- Bing's AI claims answer cites the broker's contact page as a source (AI-03).

## 5. Cancellation / MTA / change-car difficulty (Category H)

  Cancellations Department… 0330 118 0404" in the snippet (SE-05) — a Hello Prima
  customer trying to cancel is shown the broker's cancellations department.
