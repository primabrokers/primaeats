# Agent 5 — Aggregator / Comparison Site Investigation (AGG-)

Date of research: 2026-07-02 (times approximate, 00:45–01:45 UTC).
Scope: Go.Compare, Compare the Market, Confused.com, MoneySuperMarket, Quotezone, Uswitch, Mustard, Money.co.uk, Which?, Defaqto — plus third-party comparison/contact-lookup sites surfaced by the brief's Agent 5 queries. Public pages only; no quote journeys were run and no personal data was entered.

## Summary

- **No aggregator page was found displaying Prima Insurance Brokers' phone numbers (0330 088 1135 / 0161 826 1620), email, or Prestwich address in connection with Hello Prima / Prima car insurance.** This is a clear negative finding for the ten in-scope aggregators. Searches for those numbers restricted to gocompare.com and the other aggregator domains returned nothing (AGG-12).
- **However, a live third-party "contact page" site (kaeltripton.com) — surfaced by the exact query pattern the brief targets — publishes a page titled "Prima Car Insurance Contact UK 2026: Phone, Claims & Complaints" that repeatedly directs consumers to primainsurance.co.uk (the broker's website) as "Prima's official site" for customer service, 24/7 claims and complaints. It never mentions helloprima.co.uk.** Anyone following it for Hello Prima contact/claims/complaints lands on Prima Insurance Brokers' contact page, which displays 0330 088 1135. This is the strongest routing-confusion evidence found by this agent (AGG-08, Confirmed).
- Four of the ten in-scope aggregators publicly list the insurer, and **none of them uses the trading name "Hello Prima"**: Go.Compare lists "Prima Car Insurance"; Confused.com and Uswitch list "Prima", "Prima Essentials", "Prima Plus", "Prima Premier". All are bare names with **no contact details, no link, and no disambiguation** from Prima Insurance Brokers (AGG-01, AGG-03, AGG-04). This is aggregator ambiguity (Category J): the consumer is handed the brand word "Prima" and left to find contact details by searching.
- Go.Compare has listed "Prima Car Insurance" in its providers directory since at least 25 May 2025 (Wayback), and its own contact page states "Go.Compare is a comparison website and not an insurer or a broker... you buy it from the insurer" — i.e. post-sale queries are pushed to "Prima", with no Prima contact details supplied anywhere on gocompare.com (AGG-02, AGG-07).
- Compare the Market: **no public page mentioning Prima could be found or captured.** Live pages (trusted-partners, car-insurance/providers) are Cloudflare-blocked to automated capture, nothing is indexed in Bing/DDG for site:comparethemarket.com "Prima", and there are zero Wayback snapshots of those URLs. Trustpilot reviews of Hello Prima state customers bought via Compare the Market, so the relationship exists but is invisible on public pages (AGG-05; access blocker).
- MoneySuperMarket's car-insurance providers directory does **not** list Prima at all, despite Trustpilot reviewers saying they bought Prima via MoneySuperMarket — the consumer again has no aggregator-published contact route (AGG-06).
- Quotezone, Mustard, Money.co.uk, Which? and Defaqto: **no public page mentioning Hello Prima / Prima car insurance found** (Quotezone's provider list captured and grep-verified; Which? "best and worst car insurance" reviews 25 insurers, Prima not among them; Defaqto's only indexed "Prima" content is Margetts Prima investment funds, even though helloprima.co.uk advertises "5 Star Defaqto" ratings).
- Secondary comparison/claims sites found via the brief's queries: claimsnumber.com publishes a "Prima Car Insurance Accident Claim Contact Phone Number" page pushing **0333 006 4465**, a claims-management company (ClaimsContact.com), not Hello Prima — third-party claims-routing ambiguity (AGG-09). freepricecompare.com and fairerfinance.com use the bare brand "Prima" with no contact details (AGG-10, AGG-11).
- Search-journey note: the query «"Go.Compare"/"GoCompare" "Hello Prima" contact phone number» surfaced primainsurance.co.uk/contact and prima-finance.co.uk/contact among top results — consistent with Agent 1's search-ambiguity findings (Category I context).
- Honest gaps: aggregator quote journeys and post-sale account areas were not tested (out of bounds); Compare the Market could not be captured at all; DuckDuckGo returned no organic results for several site: queries (Bing used instead).
